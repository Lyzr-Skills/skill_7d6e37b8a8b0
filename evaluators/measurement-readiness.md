# Measurement Readiness Evaluator

Runs a measurement preflight checklist before campaign activation.

## Checklist
- **Objective defined**: Yes/No
- **Primary metric**: Yes/No
- **Conversion event**: Yes/No
- **Tracking verified**: Yes/No
- **Attribution verified**: Yes/No
- **Baseline set**: Yes/No
- **Target set**: Yes/No
- **Dashboard configured**: Yes/No

## Status Assessment
- **READY**: All checklist items are verified (Yes).
- **BLOCKED**: If any checklist item is "No". The campaign cannot launch because performance cannot be evaluated.
