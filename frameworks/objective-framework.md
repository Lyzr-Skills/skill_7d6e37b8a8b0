# Objective Classification Framework

Forces mapping from vague objectives to precise behaviors, conversion events, and metrics.

## Objective Categories
1. **Awareness**: High-level exposure, reach.
2. **Engagement**: User interaction, content consumption.
3. **Lead generation**: Capturing contact details.
4. **Pipeline generation**: Generating qualified sales opportunities.
5. **Conversion**: Free-to-paid, purchase completion.
6. **Activation**: First-use milestones.
7. **Retention**: Ongoing usage, low churn.
8. **Expansion**: Upsell, cross-sell.
9. **Reactivation**: Re-engaging churned users.

## Mapping Table

| Objective | Desired Behavior | Conversion Event | Primary Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pipeline Generation** | ICP books a demo | Qualified demo booked | Qualified Demos |
| **Lead Generation** | Download premium asset | Lead form submitted | Total Leads |
| **Awareness** | Read detailed report | Page-view of report | Page Views |
| **Activation** | Complete workspace setup | First successful integration | Active Workspaces |
| **Retention** | Log in 3 times/week | Weekly active milestone met | WAU / Retention rate |
